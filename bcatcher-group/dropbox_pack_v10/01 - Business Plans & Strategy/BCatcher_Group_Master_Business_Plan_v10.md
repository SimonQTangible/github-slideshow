## 1. Executive summary
B-Catcher Group is an Australian wholesale private-markets group made of three companies with one controlled lifecycle: **B-Catcher** (parent) originates, structures and manages property trusts, private credit and private equity / pre-IPO investments; **Honey Pot** recruits, accredits and supervises the broker network that distributes approved offers; **BHive** is the single investor-facing platform where investors are verified, subscribe, hold, receive reporting and, once licensed, trade on a secondary market.

The group needs **$4,637,325** of one-off set-up and build spend and a total of **$11.71m** of cumulative funding before it becomes cash-flow positive in **Jul 2029**. It reaches positive EBITDA in **Jul 2029** and, by 2031, **$28.76m** revenue, **$13.29m** EBITDA and **$756.98m** of managed AUM with **11** FTE.

## 3. Products and fees
v9 fees: the BHive entry and management fees are kept; the technology fee is cut to 0.25% and the property placement fee to 2.5%, so the ongoing fee on property is 1.5% p.a. all-in. Every offer shows one total-cost figure. Honey Pot's placement fee funds broker introduction commissions. See the Investor Returns sheet: investors keep about 70-79% of gross returns.

| Product | Mix | Honey Pot placement fee (broker commissions) | B-Catcher entry fee | B-Catcher management fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Property trusts / tokens (geared and Debt-Free series) | 45% | 2.5% (brokers 1.5%) | 1.5% (BHive) | 1.25% p.a. (BHive) |
| Private credit | 25% | 1.5% (brokers 0.9%) | 1.5% | 1.00% p.a. |
| Private equity / pre-IPO (UTEGBO trusts) | 20% | 5.0% (brokers 3.0%) | 1.5% | 1.50% p.a. |
| ASX placements / IPO | 10% | 4.0% | - | - |

BHive charges its 0.25% p.a. technology fee, $30,000 per offer onboarded and a 0.25% exchange fee per trade on 15% annual turnover (40% to the licensed operator). **Buy-back structure (v9):** the buy-back is the investors' exit route until the secondary market opens - available from year 2 for property tokens (20% / 15% / 10% / 5% below valuation by holding year) and PE trust units (10% / 8% / 5%), discretionary and capped at 5% of eligible units a year. Once the secondary market is live (month 31) requests go there first and the group buys only the residual. Resale assumes a price haircut, and a 1.5% cash reserve is held on top of the minimum buffer. Brokers source 70% of capital and receive 60% of the placement fee. Performance fees are excluded.

The loan facility is venture debt or a working-capital line at 12% p.a. plus a 2% fee. It is interest-only until month 37 (Jan 2030), then repaid over 24 months from operating cash flow, and fully repaid by Dec 2031. Total interest and fees: $1,260,000. Replacing Series B with debt keeps the founders well above 51% and frees room for the key-people share plan. Ask the lender for a **$2m accordion** (extra limit on request) to absorb small shocks; a 6-month licensing or broker delay, or the Downside, would still need the board pivot gate and new money.

Founders and legacy B-Catcher/BHive holders keep 58.3% of the shares and **59.8% of the votes** after Seed and Series A (OK - founders hold 59.8% of votes; floor 51% of votes). All key-people equity is **non-voting**: 25% of Honey Pot, 25% of BHive and a 3% group pool (see *04 - Organisation & Headcount / Key_People_Share_Plan_v3.md*). Lowest month-end cash with this plan: $1,213,305 (buffer $750k).

**Board pivot gate (month 18):** if run-rate placements are below ~$25m p.a. (about 80% of plan), hold the volume-triggered hires, cut outsourced and build-partner spend, defer the secondary-market build and stay under the principal licensee longer. At downside volumes the full three-company build does not pay for itself.

## 9. Governance and risk
Board with independent Chair (month 1) and two NEDs (month 13); independent investment-committee member (month 7); Head of Risk & Compliance reporting to the board. Key risks: regulatory perimeter, placement volume, broker misconduct, liquidity expectations (no guaranteed exits), client money reconciliation, cyber. See Launch Playbook for gates.
