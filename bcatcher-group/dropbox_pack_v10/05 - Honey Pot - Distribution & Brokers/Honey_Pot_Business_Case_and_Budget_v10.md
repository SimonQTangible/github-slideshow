## Role in the group
Honey Pot is the distribution engine: it pays away most of its fees to brokers and its strategic value is the capital and AUM it feeds to B-Catcher and BHive. It runs as a cost centre at a loss until scale, while the group consolidates to profit.

## Commission & fee schedule

Commissions are paid only on cleared, accepted subscriptions; clawback on refunds/cancellations; GST separately; dual authorisation and monthly reconciliation. 30% of capital is direct (no commission).

Be smart. Launch mechanic: *The First Hive* - 500 Founding Members. v10 adds a $15k/month national growth layer (media, PR, partnerships) from month 13 on top of performance marketing at 0.15% of capital placed.

## Honey Pot Partner Program (broker tiers)
| Tier | Who | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Worker | Newly accredited brokers and referrers | Portal access, training, standard commission on cleared money |
| Builder | Brokers with consistent placements | Priority allocation on new offers, co-marketing support, quarterly reviews |
| Queen | Top-producing partners | First look at deals, joint events, dedicated relationship manager |

Commissions are paid only on cleared, accepted subscriptions and stay inside the placement fee; tier benefits are service and access, never extra conflicted payments.
