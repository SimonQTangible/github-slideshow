### 2. What Honey Pot offers

- **Commission:** 60% of the placement fee, paid only on subscriptions that have cleared and been accepted, with clawback on refunds. Indicatively, this is 1.5% of capital for property, 0.9% for private credit, 3.0% for PE / pre-IPO and 2.4% for ASX placements. Final terms are set out in the broker agreement.
- **Access:** the BHive broker portal, approved offer documents, lead attribution through your own referral link, commission statements and investor reporting.
- **Partner tiers:**
  - **Worker:** newly accredited brokers.
  - **Builder:** priority allocations and co-marketing.
  - **Queen:** first look at deals, joint events, a dedicated relationship manager, and eligibility for **Honey Pot partner equity** (non-voting shares that vest over 4 years on tenure and quality measures).
- **Training:** accreditation and product training, plus professional-development events.

### 3. Conditions

Your participation depends on:

- (a) completing Honey Pot's broker due diligence and accreditation, including verification of your licence status, training and a signed broker agreement
- (b) Honey Pot holding the required authorisations, either as a corporate authorised representative or under its own AFSL
- (c) offers being approved and available on BHive
- (d) ongoing compliance with Honey Pot's supervision, file-review and marketing rules

### 4. Non-binding

This letter states present intention only. It is not:

- a binding agreement
- an offer of securities

- a promise of any commission, allocation or equity
