## Slide 2: Private markets are hard to reach, hard to sell and hard to exit
- Wholesale investors want property, private credit and pre-IPO exposure; the path to it is fragmented and manual
- Access
- Quality private deals reach investors through informal networks, paper applications and inconsistent eligibility checks.
- Distribution
- Brokers and advisers lack one compliant channel: no shared offer library, attribution or commission ledger.
- Liquidity
- Once invested, holders have no visibility of their portfolio and no structured way to transfer interests.
- Market sizing and competitor evidence to be independently verified before external circulation (Deck Content Brief).

## Slide 4: Three companies, one lifecycle
- Each entity owns one job and earns its own revenue - so each can be licensed, staffed and valued on its own
- B-Catcher
- Parent - originate & manage
- Sources and structures property trusts, private credit, PE / pre-IPO / Investment committee, asset management, group finance and risk / Earns entry and management fees (BHive fee schedule)
- Honey Pot
- Distribution & broker network
- Recruits, accredits and supervises brokers / Approves offers for distribution; owns lead attribution / Earns placement fees; pays broker commissions
- BHive
- Investor platform & marketplace
- Onboarding, KYC, wholesale verification, e-sign / Subscriptions, wallet, reporting, secondary market / Earns technology, onboarding, exchange and buy-back resale fees
- Investors subscribe directly through BHive. Honey Pot manages brokers and distribution behind that process. B-Catcher originates and manages the investments.

## Slide 5: Client money and assets sit outside the operating group
- Each investment is a separate trust or SPV; licensed third parties hold assets and run regulated roles until the group holds its own permissions
- B-Catcher Group (parent) / Strategy · origination · asset management

- BHive Pty Ltd / Platform · wallet · marketplace tech
- Investment trusts / SPVs / Hold assets · issue units · trust debt
- Principal licensee / RE / regulated roles until own AFSL
- Trustee & custodian / holds legal title and client money
- Fund admin & registry / authoritative unit register
- Licensed market operator / executes secondary trades
- External regulated counterparties (contracted)
- Subscriptions and trust assets are never group revenue or group cash.

- Disclosed pricing
- Bids, offers and dated valuations shown to verified buyers
- Licensed execution
- Matching by authorised operator; partner takes 40% of fees
- Register + wallet update together
- Settlement reconciled to the legal register; full audit trail
- $48.3m

- Buy-back windows
