## 1. Executive summary
B-Catcher Group is an Australian wholesale private-markets group made of three companies with one controlled lifecycle: **B-Catcher** (parent) originates, structures and manages property trusts, private credit and private equity / pre-IPO investments; **Honey Pot** recruits, accredits and supervises the broker network that distributes approved offers; **BHive** is the single investor-facing platform where investors are verified, subscribe, hold, receive reporting and, once licensed, trade on a secondary market.

The group needs **$3,382,500** of one-off set-up and build spend and a total of **$9.18m** of cumulative funding before it becomes cash-flow positive in **Oct 2029**. It reaches positive EBITDA in **Jul 2029** and, by 2031, **$22.47m** revenue, **$11.60m** EBITDA and **$513.63m** of managed AUM with **5** FTE.

| Investment trusts / SPVs | Hold assets, issue units, carry trust-level debt | N/A - investor money and assets are never group revenue or group cash | External trustee, custodian, registry |

## 3. Products and fees
Investor fees follow the BHive Capital fee schedule. Honey Pot's placement fee, paid by the issuer, funds broker introduction commissions.

| Product | Mix | Honey Pot placement fee (broker commissions) | B-Catcher entry fee | B-Catcher management fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Property trusts / tokens | 45% | 3.0% (brokers 1.8%) | 1.5% (BHive) | 1.25% p.a. (BHive) |
| Private credit | 25% | 1.5% (brokers 0.9%) | 1.5% | 1.00% p.a. |
| Private equity / pre-IPO (UTEGBO trusts) | 20% | 5.0% (brokers 3.0%) | 1.5% | 1.50% p.a. |
| ASX placements / IPO | 10% | 4.0% | - | - |

BHive charges its 0.50% p.a. technology fee, $20,000 per offer onboarded and a 0.25% exchange fee per trade on 15% annual turnover (40% to the licensed operator). **Buy-back structure kept:** property tokens follow the BHive exit ladder (25% / 15% / 10% below independent valuation in years 3-5) and PE trust units follow the UTEGBO windows (15% / 10% / 8%). The group buys units back, resells them at valuation and holds a funded cash reserve of 2% of eligible AUM. Brokers source 70% of capital and receive 60% of the placement fee. Performance fees are excluded.

## 9. Governance and risk

Key risks: regulatory perimeter, placement volume, broker misconduct, liquidity expectations (no guaranteed exits), client money reconciliation, cyber. See Launch Playbook for gates.
