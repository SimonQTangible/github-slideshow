## 1. Executive summary
B-Catcher Group is an Australian wholesale private-markets group made of three companies with one controlled lifecycle: **B-Catcher** (parent) originates, structures and manages property trusts, private credit and private equity / pre-IPO investments; **Honey Pot** recruits, accredits and supervises the broker network that distributes approved offers; **BHive** is the single investor-facing platform where investors are verified, subscribe, hold, receive reporting and, once licensed, trade on a secondary market.

The group needs **$3,338,500** of one-off set-up and build spend and a total of **$11.21m** of cumulative funding before it becomes cash-flow positive in **Aug 2029**. It reaches positive EBITDA in **Aug 2029** and, by 2031, **$21.39m** revenue, **$7.03m** EBITDA and **$513.63m** of managed AUM with **24** FTE.

## 2. Group structure
| Entity | Role | Revenue | Regulatory route |
|---|---|---|---|
| B-Catcher (parent) | Origination, investment committee, asset management, group finance, risk, shared services | Property acquisition fee, structuring fees, management fees; intercompany shared-services fee | Manager under external trustee/RE; no client money |
| Honey Pot | Broker recruitment, accreditation, supervision, offer approval, lead attribution, commissions | Placement fees from issuers (net of broker commissions) | CAR under principal licensee from month 4; own AFSL application months 13-20 |
| BHive | Investor, broker and admin portals; wallet; reporting; secondary market | Platform admin fee on AUM, offer onboarding fees, secondary transaction fees; intercompany licence fees | Technology provider; trading via licensed market-operator partner from month 19 |
| Investment trusts / SPVs | Hold assets, issue units, carry trust-level debt | N/A - investor money and assets are never group revenue or group cash | External trustee, custodian, registry |

## 9. Governance and risk
Board with independent Chair (month 1) and two NEDs (month 13); independent investment-committee member (month 7); Head of Risk & Compliance reporting to the board. Key risks: regulatory perimeter, placement volume, broker misconduct, liquidity expectations (no guaranteed exits), client money reconciliation, cyber. See Launch Playbook for gates.
