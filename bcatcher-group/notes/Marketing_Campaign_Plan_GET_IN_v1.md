## 1. The campaign in one line

> **GET IN.**
> *The deals that build real wealth used to be invite-only. Now you're in.*

**Why "GET IN" wins:**
- It's two words, active and confident, and it works as a headline, a button ("Get in"), a hashtag (#GetIn) and a community ("Are you in?").
- It carries every pillar: **get in** on property, **get in** before the float, **get in** on the lender's side, **get in** with AI on your side, **get in** with the people who know.
- It flips exclusivity into invitation, which fits the wholesale-only reality: you have to qualify to get in.
- It sets up a natural second message for later: *"...and get out when you need to"* (secondary market and buy-back windows).

### The campaign system
| Line | Pillar | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **GET IN.** | Master | Every asset |
| **Get in on property.** *Own the building, not the mortgage.* | Fractional property | Property offers, property-investor persona |
| **Get in before the bell.** | Pre-IPO / IPO / placements | Capital-markets offers, business owners and professionals |
| **Get in on the lender's side.** *Be the bank.* | Private credit | SMSF trustees, income seekers |
| **Get in with AI on your side.** | AI deal sourcing | Next-gen, tech-curious, PR |
| **Get in with people who know.** | Team & investment committee | Trust-building, brokers |
| **Get in. Get out. Your call.** | Secondary market / buy-back | From month 19 (when live) |
| **Get in. See everything.** | Transparent fees / app | Comparison and credibility ads |

### Brand architecture (what the customer sees)
- **BHive** is the consumer brand: the app, the community ("the Hive") and the ads. Investors join *BHive*.
- **B-Catcher** is the manager and the trust behind it, shown as "Investments managed by B-Catcher": the credibility sign-off.
- **Honey Pot** is the partner brand for brokers and advisers: the *Honey Pot Partner Program*.
- **The metaphor:** a hive is collective power. Thousands of investors pooling together can buy what only institutions could. **"Alone, you buy a unit. Together, the Hive buys the building."** Use it in hero film and explainers. Avoid "honey = returns" imagery that implies easy or sweet profits.

## 3. The three big creative ideas

### Big Idea 1 - "The Invitation" (pre-launch and launch)
A physical and digital invitation-only launch. Every verified wholesale investor receives a numbered **BHive Founding Member** invitation (#0001 to #0500). Numbers are scarce and public on a live counter. Founding Members get early access to the first three offers, a no-onboarding-fee launch period, a founders' dinner and a lifetime "Founding Member" badge in the app.
- **Hero line:** *"For 50 years, the best deals came with an invitation. Here's yours."*
- **Mechanic:** a "Do you qualify?" 60-second quiz, then accountant certificate upload or verification, then your number.
- **Why it works:** scarcity, status and referral ("I'm #0047"), and it fits wholesale gating naturally.

### Big Idea 2 - "Alone vs The Hive" (brand film and always-on)
A split-screen story. On the left, one person trying to buy an investment property: the bank rejection, the deposit, the stamp duty, the tenant call at 2am. On the right, the same person on BHive: the AI shortlist, the expert check, owning a share of three buildings, income landing in the wallet. End frame: **"Alone, you buy a unit. Together, the Hive buys the building. GET IN."**
- **Formats:** a 60-second hero film (YouTube, LinkedIn, cinema-pre-roll optional), 15/6-second cut-downs, carousel versions.
- **Spin-offs:** "Alone vs The Hive: Lending" (the bank earns the margin vs you earn the margin) and "Alone vs The Hive: IPO" (reading about it after the float vs getting in before the bell).

### Big Idea 3 - "Proof, Not Promises" (always-on content engine)
A content series that shows the edge instead of claiming it.
- **Deal Teardown** (weekly, 90 seconds): the CIO and AI screen a real deal live. "Here's why we bought it, and here's what could go wrong."
- **Rejected Deals** (monthly): "We said no to 47 deals this month. Here are three, and why." Nothing builds trust faster.
- **AI Deal Index** (quarterly report and PR hook): below-valuation opportunities by city and asset type. It makes B-Catcher the source journalists quote.
- **Income Day** (monthly, once live): aggregated distributions paid. "This month the Hive paid $X to N investors."
- **Ask the Lender** (fortnightly short): how private credit works, LVRs, security and what happens if a loan defaults.

### "World's best" needs an Accelerate option
The model budget is a lean launch. To build a category-leading brand, I recommend an **Accelerate** layer funded only if Founding Member targets are hit (a gated spend):

| Layer | Year 1 | Year 2 | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base (in model) | about $0.37m | about $0.38m | - |

| Accelerate: PR agency retainer | $60k | $72k | Launch month |
| Accelerate: events (founders' dinners, Hive nights) | $40k | $80k | First 3 offers placed |
| **Accelerate total** | **$370k** | **$452k** | Gated |

### Unit economics: what we can afford to pay for an investor
- Average first investment: **$150,000**.
- Group revenue from that investment: about **5% up front ($7,500)** plus about **1.75% a year ($2,600)** while held.
- First-investment payback means we can afford **up to about $2,000-2,500 to acquire a verified investor who invests**.
- Funnel assumption: quiz start → verified **25%** → first investment **30%**. That puts the target cost per verified investor at **≤ $600** and the cost per quiz start at **≤ $150**.
- Every Accelerate dollar should be judged against these numbers monthly. If costs run 2x over target, cut paid media and lean on partners and referrals.

## 13. Compliance built into the campaign

- **ASIC RG 234 (updated 9 June 2026):** applies to all ads, including AI-generated content. Every asset gets human review, sign-off by the Head of Risk and an entry in the ad register.
- **Risk balance:** every benefit is shown with a risk statement of similar prominence. No "guaranteed", "safe", "risk-free" or "liquid".
- **Targets, not promises:** target returns are labelled as targets with their basis; no past-performance claims until there is a track record, and then with the standard warning.
- **Wholesale gating:** specific offers are shown only to verified investors. Public ads use the brand, education and the quiz.
- **Claim substantiation:** an evidence file for each claim (AI screening counts, rejection rates, below-valuation purchases, team credentials).
- **Testimonials:** consented, reviewed, with no return figures; influencers and finfluencers must be properly licensed or not used (ASIC guidance on finfluencers).
- **Wording rules:** "without a mortgage in your name" (not "no interest" unless the trust is ungeared); "early access" (never "inside running"); "earn like a lender" plus "not a bank deposit".

- **Task:** launch BHive by B-Catcher with the GET IN campaign.
