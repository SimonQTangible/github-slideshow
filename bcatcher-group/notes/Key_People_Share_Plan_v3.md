# Key-People Share Plan & Funding Structure - Strategy v3

**B-Catcher Group** | 04 - Organisation & Headcount | Version 3 | 23 November 2026
This plan explains how B-Catcher Group uses equity to recruit senior people from established finance firms (C-suite, senior brokers, platform leaders) **while the founders keep voting control**. v3 replaces v2. The founders confirmed that the 51% rule is about **votes**, and that key-people equity should mainly dilute **Honey Pot and BHive**, not the parent. Figures come from *BCatcher_Group_Integrated_Financial_Model_v10.xlsx*. This is a planning document, not legal or tax advice, and the structure needs sign-off from a lawyer and a tax adviser.

---

## 1. The principles

1. **Founders keep the votes.** Every share or option given to key people is **non-voting**. The model checks that founders hold at least 51% of B-Catcher Group's votes. It now shows **59.8%**.
2. **Dilute the subsidiaries, not the parent.** Most key-people equity is in **Honey Pot (25%)** and **BHive (25%)**. B-Catcher Group keeps 75% of each and **100% of their votes**, so the founders control all three companies.
3. **Only a small parent pool (3%)** is kept, for group executives whose job spans all three companies (CFO/COO, Head of Investments).
4. **Debt instead of a third equity round.** A $4m loan facility replaces Series B, so founders and staff are diluted less.

**Investment and risk team in B-Catcher** (Head of Risk, Investment Analyst, Asset Manager, Compliance, Fund Ops) get **non-dilutive rewards**, because their work sits in the parent:
- a share of any fund performance fees (e.g. 20% of performance fees to an investment-team pool)
- phantom shares that pay cash at exit
- deferred retention bonuses

If parent equity is needed for them, it comes from the 0.5% reserve, or a top-up of non-voting parent shares, which does not affect the founders' votes.

## 5. Terms

- **Vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff, then monthly. Sign-on "buy-out" awards (faster vesting or part cash) can replace bonuses or shares a recruit gives up at a big firm.
- **Broker equity vesting:** based on tenure plus quality:
  - 3-year placement volume
  - clean file reviews
  - no compliance breaches
  - investor retention

  Volume alone does not earn equity. This avoids conflicted-remuneration risk: the group serves only wholesale clients at launch, but rewards still should not look like sales commissions.
- **Leavers:**
  - Good leavers keep vested equity, and the company has a buy-back right at fair value.
  - Bad leavers forfeit.
  - The company can claw back equity for misconduct.
- **Change of control:** vesting speeds up only if the holder is also let go after the sale (double trigger).
- **Getting value out of subsidiary shares:**
  - Holders are bought out in cash at an independent valuation, or swapped into **non-voting parent shares**, at a sale or IPO of the group.
  - From year 5, holders and the company have put and call options at fair value.
  - Drag-along and tag-along rights apply.
- **Protecting minority holders:** the fees charged between the companies must stay at arm's length:
  - B-Catcher's shared-services fee to Honey Pot and BHive
  - BHive's platform licence fees

  Holding 25% of a subsidiary is only fair if that subsidiary keeps its fair share of profit. The board reviews these fees each year, with an independent transfer-pricing check (see the valuation brief).

## 6. Tax and legal points to confirm (Australia)

- **Startup tax concession (Division 83A, ITAA 1997):** for employees of unlisted companies under 10 years old with turnover under $50m.
  - Options must be priced at or above market value, or shares issued at a discount of 15% or less, and held for 3 years.
  - Tax is generally deferred until the shares are sold, and then taxed as a capital gain.
  - Each company granting equity (the parent, Honey Pot and BHive) needs an independent market valuation at each grant round. This is budgeted at $1,500 a month in the model.
- **Employee share scheme relief (Corporations Act Part 7.12, Division 1A):** relief is available for offers to employees and eligible contractors of unlisted companies. Value caps apply where the participant pays money for the shares.
- **Non-employee brokers** may not qualify for the startup tax concession. Get specialist advice. Options include a loan-funded share plan or performance rights.
- **Constitutions:** each company needs a constitution with a non-voting B class, plus share-plan rules. This is about $25k for the two subsidiaries on top of the $40k parent budget.
- **Loan facility:** the lender takes security over company assets only. Client money and trust assets are never security.
