## 3. Every fix, mapped to the review

| # | Review finding | v8 fix | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Volume assumptions unproven | Base run-rates cut from $25/75/150/240/340m to **$20/60/120/200/280m**. Seed condition: 2-3 signed anchor issuers. | Inputs: CapY1-5 |
| 2 | Buy-back could bankrupt the group | **Secondary market first** (30 days); the group buys only the residual, **discretionary, capped at 5% of eligible units a year**. Ladder softened to **15/10/5%** (tokens) and **10/8/5%** (trust units). Resale includes a **price haircut** (3% Base, 8.5% Downside). Reserve 1.5%, sized to the cap. | Inputs: buy-back block |
| 3 | Fee stack too heavy | Property placement fee **3.0% → 2.5%**; technology fee **0.5% → 0.25%**, so property is **1.5% p.a. all-in**. One total-cost box on every offer. | Inputs; deck slide 9 |
| 4 | No investor net return | New **Investor Returns** sheet and deck slide: net 7.0% (geared property), 5.7% (Debt-Free property), 6.9% (credit), 11.9% target (PE); the investor keeps 71-79% of gross. | Model + deck slide 10 |
| 5 | Lead with a conservative case | The Base case is now the conservative case. Upside shows the earlier ambition. | Scenarios |
| 6 | Too many small trusts | Average offer **$5m → $10m** (onboarding fee $30k): 54 offers over 5 years instead of 131. | Inputs |
| 7 | 4 people cannot run 58 offers a year | Two **volume-triggered hires**: Investment Analyst & Asset Manager (m13, $140k) and Fund Operations & Finance Officer (m19, $110k). Outsourced analysis reduced to $9k/month. | Headcount |
| 8 | Tech budget too light | MVP **$480k → $650k**, secondary engine **$350k → $450k**, build-partner retainer **$24k/$32k → $40k/$50k a month**. | Setup, Opex |
| 9 | GST not modelled | **+6% opex and +5% set-up** for irrecoverable GST (input-taxed financial supplies). | Inputs: cost factors |
| 10 | Legal gaps | New set-up items: **related-party fees & conflicts framework + RG 166 review ($45k)**; **broker conflicted-remuneration & INFO 225 digital-asset opinion ($35k)**. | Setup |
| 11 | Valuation too rich | Seed on a **SAFE/note at a $14m cap** (was $18m priced); Series A $35m pre; Series B $5m at $55m pre (month 28). Exit at **10x EBITDA / 4x revenue** (was 12x / 5x). | Inputs: funding |
| 12 | Secondary market loses money at low AUM | Activation **deferred from month 19 to month 31** (AUM ~$150m); build moved to months 22-30. | Inputs, Setup, Playbook |
| 13 | Model hygiene | Scenario "extra equity" now tests against buffer **plus** buy-back reserve; slide 9's revenue-share figure fixed; competition slide added. | Scenarios, deck |
| 14 | Entity economics | BHive licence to B-Catcher **$10k → $25k/month** (BHive runs onboarding, reporting and wallet for every fund); eliminated on consolidation, so group totals are unchanged. All three entities are EBITDA-positive by 2031. | Intercompany |

## 6. Founder decisions this version assumes
- Accept a **$19m, three-round raise** and about **52%** founder ownership before ESOP.
- Accept **lower fees** in exchange for a cleaner, more marketable product.
- Accept a **capped, discretionary buy-back** (kept, but it cannot threaten the company).
- **Market launch in year 3**, not year 2.
