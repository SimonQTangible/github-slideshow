## 1. The campaign platform

> **B-SMART.**
> *The deals that build real wealth used to be invite-only. Be smart. Join the Hive.*

**Why B-SMART works**
- **It is the brand.** B-Catcher, BHive, Honey Pot: every name already plays on "B". B-SMART makes the whole group one family of ideas instead of three logos.
- **It is a call to action** ("be smart") and an identity ("I'm B-Smart"). It works as a headline, a button ("B-Smart: check if you qualify"), a hashtag (#BSmart) and a community.
- **It is credible, not hype.** "Smart" sits with long-term wealth-building, not get-rich-quick, which suits ASIC's advertising rules.
- **It extends endlessly.** The B-prefix gives one line per product and benefit (the "B-family" below), so every new BHive offering gets its own line without a new campaign.

### The B-family (one line per pillar)
| Line | Pillar | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **B-SMART.** | Master: access to the deals that build wealth | Every asset |
| **B-AN OWNER.** *Own the building, not the mortgage.* | Fractional property (geared and Debt-Free series) | Property offers; property investors |
| **B-THE BANK.** *Earn what lenders earn.* | Private credit | SMSF trustees, income seekers |
| **B-EARLY.** *Get in before the bell.* | Pre-IPO, IPO and placements | Business owners, professionals |
| **B-INFORMED.** *Our AI catches the deals others miss. Our experts check every one.* | AI deal sourcing + investment committee | Next-gen, PR |
| **B-FREE.** *Not locked in.* | Buy-back bridge now, secondary market from year 3 | Objection-handling; from month 19 |
| **B-SURE.** *Every fee, shown in dollars.* | Transparency, total-cost box | Comparison and trust ads |
| **B-CATCHER. We catch the deals. You B-Smart.** | Manager sign-off | End frame on every ad |

### Brand architecture
- **BHive** is the consumer brand: the app, the community ("the Hive") and the ads. Investors "join the Hive".
- **B-Catcher** is the manager sign-off: *"Investments caught, checked and managed by B-Catcher."*
- **Honey Pot** is the partner brand for brokers and advisers: the *Honey Pot Partner Program* (tiers: **Worker → Builder → Queen**).
- **The metaphor:** a hive is collective power. *"Alone, you buy a unit. Together, the Hive buys the building."* Avoid "honey = easy returns" imagery: honey stands for the network and the partners, never for promised profit.

## 2. The three big creative ideas (bee-themed)

### Idea 1 - "The First Hive" (pre-launch and launch)
An invitation-only launch for the first **500 Founding Members** of the Hive (#001-#500), shown as hexagon badges in the app. Founding Members get first access to the first three offers, no onboarding fee in the launch period, a founders' evening and a permanent Founding Member badge.
- **Hero line:** *"For 50 years the best deals came by invitation. B-Smart: here's yours."*
- **Mechanic:** a 60-second "Are you B-Smart?" quiz (do you qualify as a wholesale investor?) → verification → your hexagon number.

### Idea 2 - "Alone vs The Hive" (brand film and always-on)
Split screen. Left: one person trying to buy an investment property alone (bank says no, deposit, stamp duty, the 2am tenant call). Right: the same person in BHive: B-Catcher's AI flags a deal, the experts check it, they own a share of three buildings and income lands in the wallet. End frame: **"Alone, you buy a unit. Together, the Hive buys the building. B-SMART."**
Spin-offs: *Alone vs The Hive: Lending* ("the bank keeps the margin" vs "B-the bank") and *Alone vs The Hive: IPO* ("read about it after the float" vs "B-early").

### Idea 3 - "All Proof. No Buzz." (always-on content engine)
Shows the edge instead of claiming it. The bee pun carries the brand; the content carries the credibility.
- **"Caught This Week"** (weekly, 90 seconds): what B-Catcher's AI caught, what the investment committee did with it.
- **"Let Go"** (monthly): the deals we rejected, and why. "We let 9 out of 10 go."
- **"The Hive Index"** (quarterly report, PR hook): below-valuation opportunities by city and asset type.
- **"Hive Income Day"** (monthly, once live): aggregated distributions paid to the Hive.
- **"Ask B-The-Bank"** (fortnightly): how private credit works, LVRs, security, what happens in a default.

## 5. Compliance wording (unchanged, applied to B-lines)
| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "B-rich" / "guaranteed returns" | "B-Smart": positioning, not a promise. "Target return X% (not guaranteed). Capital at risk." |
| "B-the bank" alone | "B-the bank: earn like a lender through our private-credit funds. Not a deposit; not government guaranteed." |
| "B-early: inside running" | "B-early: early access to pre-IPO and placement allocations, subject to availability" |
| "B-free: exit anytime" | "B-free: ways to exit - capped buy-back now, secondary market from year 3 (conditions apply)" |
| "Own property with no interest" | "Own a share without a mortgage in your name" (or the Debt-Free series) |
