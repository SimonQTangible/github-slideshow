# Key-People Share Plan & Funding Structure - Strategy v2

**B-Catcher Group** | 04 - Organisation & Headcount | Version 2 | 23 November 2026
How B-Catcher Group uses equity to attract senior people from established finance firms (C-suite, senior brokers, platform leaders) **while the founders always keep at least 51% of the parent company**. v2 replaces v1. The raise is now smaller: Seed $6m + Series A $6m, plus a **$4m loan facility instead of a Series B**. That frees room for a much larger share pool. The figures come from *BCatcher_Group_Integrated_Financial_Model_v10.xlsx*. This is a planning document. It is not legal or tax advice, and the structure needs sign-off from a lawyer and a tax adviser.

## 1. The funding rethink: debt instead of a third equity round

|---|---|---|
| Seed | $6m at $14m cap | $6m at $14m cap |

| Series B | $5m at $55m pre | **None** |

| **Founders' ownership** | 51.1% | **53.9%** ✔ (about 3 points above the floor) |
| Seed / Series A return (illustrative) | 6.4x / 3.7x | **7.1x (~48% IRR) / 3.6x (~35%)** |
| Lowest cash | $1.21m | $1.21m (Sep 2027); funding check passes |
| Interest and fees over 5 years | - | $1.26m |

**Why it works:**
- Debt does not dilute anyone, so every dollar borrowed instead of raised as equity stays with the founders and the share pool.
- The facility is drawn only after Series A, when the platform is live and fees are coming in. That is when venture lenders will lend, typically up to about a third of the equity raised.
- It is repaid from operating cash flow in 2030-31, after the group turns cash-flow positive in Jul 2029.

**The trade-off (be upfront with investors):**
- Less equity means less spare cash. In the Base case, cash stays only about $0.13m above the minimum buffer at its lowest point.
- Negotiate a **$2m accordion**: the right to borrow more from the same lender on request. It covers small shocks, such as a 25% technology overrun (+$0.5m).
- A 6-month licensing delay (+$4.6m), a 6-month broker delay (+$2.8m) or the Downside case would still need new money and the month-18 pivot gate.
- Any new equity must respect the 51% founder floor. That makes the accordion and early cost cuts the first lines of defence.
- Lenders usually ask for a small warrant (a right to buy shares later). The 3 points of founder headroom are kept partly for this.

## 4. Protecting the 51%

- The shareholders' agreement must include:
  - founder consent for any share issue that would take the founders below 51%
  - founder pre-emptive rights on new issues
  - a capped parent pool
  - non-voting subsidiary shares
- The model's **founder control check** (Funding & Returns sheet) now reads "OK - founders 53.9%". It turns to "BREACH" if a change takes them below 51%.
- **Headroom budget:** about 3 points. This covers lender warrants (~0.5-1%), a possible pool top-up, or a small emergency raise. Anything larger needs the accordion, cost cuts or non-voting shares.
- If the rule is really about **votes** rather than economic ownership, non-voting parent shares (dual class) would allow more equity for staff and investors without touching control. Please confirm which one you mean.

## 5. Terms

- **Vesting:** 4 years, 1-year cliff, then monthly. Faster vesting or partly cash "buy-out" awards can replace bonuses a recruit gives up by leaving their old employer.
- **Broker equity vesting:** tenure plus quality:
  - 3-year placement volume
  - clean file reviews
  - no compliance breaches
  - investor retention

  This avoids conflicted-remuneration risk. The ban applies to advice to retail clients, and the group serves only wholesale clients at launch, but rewards should still not look like sales commissions.
- **Leavers:** good leavers keep vested equity, with a company buy-back right at fair value. Bad leavers forfeit. Equity can be clawed back for misconduct.
- **Change of control:** unvested equity accelerates only if the holder is also let go (double trigger).
- **Subsidiary shares:**
  - At a sale or IPO, they are bought for cash at an independent valuation, or swapped into parent shares (only within the 51% floor).
  - From year 5, holders and the company have put and call options at fair value.
  - Drag-along and tag-along rights apply.
  - Agreements between the three companies must stay at arm's length to protect minority holders.

## 6. Tax and legal points to confirm (Australia)

- **Startup tax concession (Division 83A, ITAA 1997):** for employees of unlisted companies under 10 years old with turnover under $50m. Options must be at or above market value, or shares issued at a discount of 15% or less, held for 3 years. Tax is generally deferred to sale and charged as capital gains. An independent valuation is needed each grant round; this is budgeted at $1,500 a month.
- **Employee share scheme relief (Corporations Act Part 7.12, Division 1A):** covers offers to employees and eligible contractors of unlisted companies. Offers where staff pay money are subject to value caps.
- **Non-employee brokers** may not get the startup tax concession. Get specialist advice; options include a loan-funded share plan or performance rights.
- **Loan facility:** venture-debt lenders take security over company assets and set covenants (minimum cash, reporting). Client money and trust assets are never security, as they are not group assets. Confirm this with the lender and the trustee.
- **Budget:** subsidiary share-plan documents ~$25k, and facility legal costs ~$30k (covered by the 2% fee assumption plus legal contingency).
