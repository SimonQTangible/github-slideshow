# Why Us - Positioning, Edge & Marketing Strategy (v1)

**B-Catcher Group** | 09 - Notes | Working draft | 23 November 2026
Foundation for the marketing campaign. It covers why investors are looking for alternatives, what makes us different, who we are talking to, what we say, and how we say it legally. Not an offer or financial advice. All campaign copy must go through compliance review before use.

---

## 1. The big idea

> **The best deals have always gone to the insiders. Now they come to you.**

For decades, the property deals, private loans and pre-IPO rounds that build real wealth have been offered first to banks, institutions and the well connected. Everyone else got term deposits, super defaults and a mortgage. B-Catcher Group opens that door. Our AI and our team find the deals, and BHive puts them in your pocket: you own a share, you earn like a lender, you get in before the float.

**Campaign line options** (test these, then pick one):
- *Stop watching the rich get richer. Invest like them.*
- *Wealth isn't luck. It's access.*
- *The deals used to be invite-only. You're invited.*
- *Own the building, not the mortgage.*
- *Be the bank.*
- *Get in before the bell.* (for pre-IPO and IPO)

"Make yourself wealthier" works better than "richer". It sounds long-term, credible and less get-rich-quick, which matters under ASIC's advertising rules.

## 2. Why people are looking for alternative investments (the "why now")

| Driver | Evidence | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Locked out of property** | Price-to-income near record highs (about 8x+ in 2025 vs a 20-year average of about 6.8). About 11 years to save a 20% deposit. A first-home buyer on $180k household income can afford about **12%** of homes for sale. Moody's expects affordability to worsen through 2026 as rates rose. | Huge appetite for property exposure without a mortgage. Fractional ownership is the answer to "I'll never afford the building". |
| **Rates went up again in 2026** | 2026 rate rises pushed repayments to about 45% of gross income for a new mortgage. | Borrowing capacity is maxed out. Investors want yield and growth without more personal debt. Private credit lets them earn the lender's side of high rates. |
| **Private markets FOMO** | Morningstar 2026: retail private equity ownership rose to **20%** (from 15%) and private debt to **10%** (from 8%). LGT: high-net-worth Australians' private-markets allocation rose from **3% to 7%** in a year. | The trend is already here. We don't have to create demand, only capture it with a better product. |
| **Institutions get better access** | Institutions and super funds have long held private equity, private credit and unlisted property. Natixis: 4 in 5 institutional investors say private markets offer stronger return potential. | Message: "the big funds already do this; now you can too". |
| **Biggest wealth transfer in history** | An estimated **$3.5-5.4 trillion** will pass from baby boomers to their children over the next 20 years. | Next-generation inheritors want control, transparency and digital access, not their parents' broker. |
| **SMSFs are huge and self-directed** | About **$1.05 trillion** in SMSFs, over 1 million members, average about $1.58m per fund. The Division 296 tax on balances over $3m is prompting reviews of structures. | SMSF trustees are our core buyers. They are already sophisticated, want income and real assets, and make their own decisions. |
| **More people qualify than ever** | About **11-12%** of households (about 1.1 million) meet the sophisticated-investor test. The thresholds have not been indexed since 2002. | A big, growing wholesale audience. Watch for threshold reform (see section 8). |
| **Distrust of banks and fees** | Persistent anger at bank margins, fees and low deposit rates. | "Be the bank" lands emotionally. We must be visibly transparent on our own fees, or we become what we criticise. |
| **Want something real** | Volatile shares, crypto swings and the sameness of index funds. | Tangible assets: a building you can visit, a loan secured on real property, a company you can name. |

**Insight:** people aren't looking for "alternative investments". They are looking for:
1. **Access** - "Why do the big players get the good deals?"
2. **Control** - "I want to choose what I own."
3. **Income** - "I need my money to pay me, not sit there."
4. **A way into property** - "I can't get a second mortgage."
5. **To feel smart and early** - "I want to be in before everyone else."

Every message should hit one of these five.

## 4. Our edge - the seven pillars

Each pillar shows the claim, the proof we have or need, and what must be true before we advertise it.

### Pillar 1 - AI that finds deals the market misses
- **Claim:** Our AI scans thousands of listings, sales, development approvals and distressed signals every day to find properties priced below independent valuation. Our team then verifies every one.
- **Proof needed:** Track the "discount to independent valuation at purchase" on every deal and publish it once we have 3+ deals. Name the data sources (CoreLogic, council DA feeds, auction results).
- **Must be true first:** We can only say "below market" after an independent valuation shows it, deal by deal. Never imply AI guarantees returns.

### Pillar 2 - A team with real industry knowledge
- **Claim:** Property, lending and capital-markets operators, not salespeople. Investment committee with an independent member.
- **Proof needed:** Bios with deal history, dollar values and years. Investment committee charter.
- **Message:** "AI finds it. Experts check it. You decide."

### Pillar 3 - Own a share of property without a mortgage
- **Claim:** Buy a piece of a property from $X with no loan in your name, no stamp duty in your name, no tenants to manage, and income paid to your wallet.
- **Important:** In the current model our property trusts carry about 45% gearing, so interest is paid inside the trust. "Without paying interest" is not accurate for geared trusts. Use **"without a mortgage of your own"** or **"no personal loan"**. Or launch an **ungeared "Debt-Free Property" series**, which makes the stronger claim true and is a powerful marketing product.

### Pillar 4 - Get in early: pre-IPO, IPO and placements
- **Claim:** Access to pre-IPO rounds, private company raises and ASX placements usually reserved for institutions and sophisticated investors.
- **Wording:** Never say "inside running" or "inside information", because it implies insider trading. Say **"early access"**, **"get in before the float"**, **"allocations usually reserved for institutions"**. Allocations are not guaranteed, and pre-IPO is high risk and illiquid.

### Pillar 5 - Be the bank: earn what lenders earn
- **Claim:** Our private-credit funds lend to property developers and businesses, secured on real assets, and pay investors a monthly income.
- **Proof needed:** Loan-to-value limits, first-mortgage security, default history, and full fee pass-through. ASIC's 2025-26 private-credit reviews criticised managers who keep borrower fees, so we make transparency our weapon.
- **Wording:** "Earn like a lender", but always alongside "not a bank deposit, not government guaranteed, capital at risk".

### Pillar 6 - You're not locked in
- **Claim:** The BHive secondary market and buy-back windows give you ways out that most private investments don't offer.
- **Proof needed:** Live only once the licensed market partner is operating. Buy-backs are discretionary and capped (see the analyst review).
- **Wording:** "Ways to exit", never "guaranteed liquidity" or "redeem anytime".

### Pillar 7 - Everything in one app, nothing hidden
- **Claim:** Onboarding, deals, documents, income, tax statements, trading and buy-back requests, all in BHive. Every fee shown in dollars before you invest.
- **Proof:** App demo, a sample statement, and a total-cost box on every offer.
- **Why it matters:** "Transparency" is our answer to the banks and to ASIC. It must be real: one total-cost figure per offer.

**Summary of the edge:** *AI-sourced deals + expert-verified + fractional ownership + early access + lender returns + ways out + one transparent app.* No Australian competitor combines all seven. Syndicators lack the app, the apps lack the deal flow, and crowdfunding platforms lack property and credit.

## 7. The wholesale tension - and how to turn it into an advantage

**Problem:** the message "stop making the rich richer" speaks to everyone, but at launch we can legally offer only to wholesale or sophisticated investors (about $2.5m net assets or $250k income for two years). Advertising wholesale products to the general public is a compliance risk.

**Solution - a two-track campaign:**
1. **Track A - Wholesale investors (revenue now).** Targeted channels: LinkedIn, accountants, brokers, SMSF communities, events. Verification gate before any offer is visible. Message: "You qualify. Most people who qualify don't know it."
   - *"Earn $250k+ or have $2.5m in assets? You may already qualify for deals most investors never see."*
2. **Track B - Everyone else (brand and waitlist for later).** General education content only, no specific offers. "The BHive Academy": how private markets work, how to read an offer, how lenders earn. Builds a waitlist for a future retail product (a registered managed investment scheme or retail fund, as a separate business case). This keeps the "make yourself wealthier" mission honest.

## 9. Compliance guardrails (built into the campaign)

- **ASIC RG 234 (updated 9 June 2026)** applies to all advertising, including **AI-generated ads**. Every ad gets human review and approval; we keep an ad register.
- Balance every benefit with a risk statement of similar prominence; no "guaranteed", "safe" or "risk-free".
- Target returns must be labelled as targets, with the basis; never use past performance as an indicator.
- Wholesale-only gating: offers are visible only after verification. Public ads promote education and the waitlist, not specific offers.
- Design and distribution obligations and target market determinations, where applicable; private-credit fee transparency (ASIC Reports 820 and 846).
- Every claim in section 4 needs a substantiation file before it runs (a "proof pack").
